Data transformation with dplyr

dplyr gives concise verbs for common tasks:

  • filter() — pick rows
  • arrange() — reorder rows
  • select() — pick columns
  • mutate() — create/transform columns
  • summarise() — collapse to summaries

Important points (highlight)

  • Always inspect your data: summary(), str(), View()
  • Watch out for sentinel values (e.g. 9999) and decide how to treat them before computing summaries
  • Missing values (NA) must be handled explicitly (na.rm or imputation)
  • dplyr verbs (filter, select, arrange, mutate, summarise) + pipes make transformation readable and reproducible
  • Use joins carefully: pick the right join (left/inner/full) and check for unexpected NAs after the join
  • When sampling or random processes, set a seed to make results reproducible
